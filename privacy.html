Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

rclone-nas-backup is a personal backup tool used only by its owner.

  • It requests only the Google Drive drive.file scope, so it can access only the files it uploaded itself, not any other files in the Drive.
  • Backup files are encrypted before they are uploaded.
  • No personal data is collected, stored anywhere else, shared, or sold. Nothing is sent to anyone other than Google Drive.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
